How delivery works

Three clear steps. No chaos. No mystery timeline.

Same process across all tiers. Tier 2 and Tier 3 add content/copy layers after visuals are locked.

1

Brief & shot list

Brand assets, offer, audience, platforms, and a locked image plan before generation starts.

2

Create & revise

Generate, human-cull, send contact sheet / concepts, then run up to 3 revision rounds.

3

Final delivery

Organized folders, usage rights, and ready-to-use assets. Tier 2/3 include content layers.

Step 1 — What we collect

  • Logo + brand colors / fonts if available
  • Offer, pricing, and CTA language
  • Target audience + platforms
  • Examples of creatives you like / hate
  • Must-include products, people, or claims
  • Hard avoids (style, claims, competitors)
  • Needed formats (feed, story, ad, web)
  • Deadline and launch date

Step 2 — Production standards

We do
  • Generate multiple options per key slot
  • Human-review for brand + conversion fit
  • Present clear A/B concept choices
  • Track revisions cleanly (3 rounds)
We don’t
  • Dump raw unreviewed AI outputs
  • Auto-post to Instagram/Facebook accounts
  • Change brand direction mid-stream without approval
  • Hold files hostage after delivery
